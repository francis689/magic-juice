MAGIC JUICE GMP POLICY

GOOD MANUFACTURING PRACTICES (GMP)

The following GMP rules must be observed in the production area:

1. Always wear a hair net that completely covers your hair.
2. Always wear a face mask that covers your mouth and nose.
3. Do not eat, drink, chew, or smoke in the production area.
4. Do not spit in the production area.
5. Wash and sanitize your hands before entering the production area.
6. Always check your body temperature before starting your work.
7. Do not use your phone in the production area.
8. Sanitize your feet before entering the production area.
9. Always observe machine rules before operating.
10. Do not use chipped or broken items in production.
11. Report all food safety issues to your supervisor.
12. Always stick to your work area.
13. Always report any damages and breakages.
14. Always be clean and well groomed. No jewellery should be worn.
15. Clean as you go.
