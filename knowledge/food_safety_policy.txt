MAGIC JUICE FOOD SAFETY POLICY

Magic Fresh Juice is totally committed to provide safe quality fresh juice to all clients and associates.

Magic is committed in this endeavour and shall:

1. CONSISTENTLY PRODUCE SAFE AND QUALITY JUICES

Consistently produce fresh quality and safe juices that are free from physical, chemical, allergen and pathogenic microbiological hazards.

This is monitored by lab results, customer complaints, and high hygiene standards.

2. EMPLOYEE TRAINING

Continuously train all employees on the impact of their role for processing safe and quality food products and communicate this policy both internally and externally in our endeavours to produce safe food products.

Training records are maintained on SOPs and Policies.

3. SUPPLIER STANDARDS

We will expect high standards from our suppliers.

This is monitored through supplier evaluation of ingredients.

4. FOOD SAFETY ENVIRONMENT

Ensuring the company environment is up to standard in controlling food safety hazards by attaining more than 80% in all GMP and food hygiene training audits.

5. LEGAL AND REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

Comply with all statutory and regulatory operation requirements.
