MAGIC JUICE LIMITED

Company Overview

Magic Juice Limited is a Kenyan juice company based in Nairobi.

The company produces and sells fresh fruit juices. Magic Juice describes its juices as 100% natural and focuses on fresh fruit-based beverages.

The company provides its products to customers and offers online ordering through its website.

Magic Juice serves customers in Nairobi and provides delivery services within Nairobi.

Location

Donholm, Nairobi, Kenya.
